Electrician Ki Qismein — Kaun Sa Banda Kis Kaam Ke Liye?

"Electrician" aik lafz hai, lekin kaam ke hisaab se bande alag hote hain. Kaam batate waqt saaf likh dein ke kis qism ka kaam hai:

  • Wiring electrician — naye ghar ya kamray ki mukammal wiring karne wala. Points, cable, board aur DB box — ye bara kaam hai, is ka estimate pehle tay hota hai.
  • Fitting aur repair wala electrician — switch, socket, fan, light aur board ki marammat karne wala. Chhota kaam aam tor par aik visit mein ho jata hai.
  • UPS / inverter wala electrician — UPS, inverter aur battery ki connection aur setting karne wala. Battery aur load ki tafseel pehle batana zaroori hoti hai.
  • Motor aur pump wala electrician — paani wali motor, pump aur boring ki wiring karne wala. Motor ki taar aur starter ka kaam alag tajurba mangta hai.
  • Electrician ka helper — electrician ke saath kaam karne wala madadgaar: taar kheenchna, saman uthana, board ki tayyari. Helper ki dihadi electrician se kam hoti hai.

Kaam ki tafseel pehle se saaf ho to banda bhi sahi milta hai aur rate bhi pehle tay ho jata hai. "Electrician chahiye" kehne se behtar hai ye likhna: "Johar Town mein 2 fan aur aik board lagwana hai, aaj shaam ko kaam hai."

Lahore Mein Sab Se Aam Bijli Ke Maslay

Bijli ka kaam plumbing se zyada ehtiyat mangta hai. Lahore mein ye maslay sab se aam hain:

  • Switch ya socket jal jana — zyada load ya purani fitting ki wajah se. Jala hua switch foran badalwana chahiye, warna aag lagne ka khatra hota hai.
  • Fan band ho jana ya awaz karna — capacitor, bearing ya wiring ka masla. Aam tor par aik visit mein theek ho jata hai.
  • Breaker bar bar trip hona — overload, kharab wiring ya kharab breaker ki nishani. Bar bar trip ho to khud theek karne ki koshish na karein.
  • Voltage ki kami ya fluctuation — kam voltage par fan aur fridge sahi nahi chalte. Wajah meter, wiring ya area ki supply bhi ho sakti hai — electrician check karke batayega.
  • Purani wiring — purane gharon mein taarain kamzor ho jati hain. Aik kamray ki wiring badalni ho ya poore ghar ki — ye bara kaam hai, estimate pehle lein.
  • UPS / inverter ki connection — battery, load aur wiring ki tafseel ke baghair connection sahi nahi lagti. Sirf wohi electrician bulayein jis ne ye kaam pehle kiya ho.
  • Meter ya main board ka masla — main board, meter ki taar ya earthing ka kaam hamesha tajurba-kaar bande se karwayein.

Bijli ke kaam mein hamesha tajurba-kaar electrician bulayein. Khud taar chhedna ya board kholna khatarnak ho sakta hai — agar short circuit ya spark nazar aaye to pehle main breaker band karein, phir bande ko bulayein.

Wiring Karwane Se Pehle Ye Tay Karein

Nayi wiring ya bara kaam karwane se pehle ye 5 batain tay kar lein — aadha masla wahin hal ho jata hai:

  1. Points ki list — kitne switch, socket, light aur fan points lagenge? Har kamray ki list pehle banwayein — points jitne zyada, kaam utna bara.
  2. Cable aur switch ki quality — cable konsi company ki aur kitne mm ki hogi? Sasti cable baad mein mehenga par sakti hai — quality pehle tay kar lein.
  3. Breaker aur load — ghar ka kul load kitna hai? AC, fridge, motor aur UPS sab ka load gin kar breaker aur cable ka size tay karwayein.
  4. Earthing (zameen wali taar) — earthing sahi ho to bijli ka jhatka aur aalat kharab hone ka khatra kam hota hai. Nayi wiring mein earthing shamil karwayein.
  5. Kaam ki hadd likh lein — theka ho to likh lein: kitne points, cable konsi, board konsa, aur kaam kab tak mukammal hoga. Likh kar tay karna behes se bachata hai.

Bijli Ke Kaam Mein Safety — Ye Batain Mat Bhoolein

Pehlay safety, phir kaam. Bijli ka kaam andaze par nahi hota — aik ghalti jaan ka khatra ban sakti hai.

  • Main switch band karwayein — kaam shuru hone se pehle main supply band karwayein aur bachon ko us jagah se door rakhein.
  • Khud taar na chhedein — switch kholna ya taar jorna aasan lagta hai, lekin ghalat connection se short circuit ya aag lag sakti hai.
  • Geeli jagah se door rahein — geele haathon ya geeli jagah par bijli ka kaam bilkul na karein. Bathroom ya kitchen ka kaam ho to pehle supply band karwayein.
  • Spark ya short circuit ho to — main breaker foran band karein, paani na dalain, aur electrician ko bulayein. Raat ko bhi GigMatch par apne area ke bande pehle dikhte hain.
  • Purani wiring ko halka na lein — agar taarain garam hoti hain ya board se jalne ki boo aati hai, to ye khatre ki nishani hai. Foran electrician bulayein.

Saman Kis Ka Hoga?

Ye sawal kaam shuru karne se pehle tay kar lein — aksar jhagra isi baat par hota hai:

  • Saman aam tor par ghar walay ka hota hai — cable, switch, board aur breaker aap khareed kar dein, aur electrician sirf mazdoori le. List pehle banwa lein taake hisaab saaf rahe.
  • Auzar electrician ke hote hain — tester, plier, drill aur seedhi zyada tar electrician apna lata hai; kaam shuru karne se pehle pooch lein.
  • Theka saman samet bhi ho sakta hai — agar electrician saman bhi de raha hai to us ki list aur rate pehle likh lein, aur receipt mangwayein.

Sahi Electrician Chunnay Ki 7 Tips

  1. Tajurba poochein — wiring aur board ka kaam hamesha tajurba-kaar bande se karwayein. Sasta nau-sikhiya mehnga par sakta hai. Poochein ke us ne aisa kaam pehle kiya hai ya nahi.
  2. Rate pehle tay karein — kaam shuru hone se pehle dihadi ya theka saaf likh ya bata dein. WhatsApp par likha hua rate baad mein behes se bachata hai.
  3. Kaam ki photo pehle bhej dein — kharab switch, board ya wiring ki tasveer WhatsApp par bhejne se banda sahi saman sath lata hai.
  4. Qareeb ka banda chunein — apne area ka electrician waqt par ata hai aur kiraya bhi bachata hai.
  5. Saman ki list pehle banwayein — cable, switch, board aur breaker jo lage, pehle likh lein taake hisaab saaf rahe aur baad mein behes na ho.
  6. Kaam ke baad check lazmi karein — har switch, socket aur fan test karein; breaker on/off karke dekhein; aur wiring ka har point chala kar check karein.
  7. Payment kaam ke baad — rate pehle tay karein, aur poori payment kaam mukammal aur check karne ke baad dein. Advance dena ho to chhoti raqam dein aur likh kar rakhein.
