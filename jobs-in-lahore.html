Frequently Asked Questions

Is signup on GigMatch PK free for jobseekers?
Yes — signup is 100% free. There is no registration fee and no commission. Workers and hirers talk to each other directly on WhatsApp.

How do I apply for a job in Lahore through GigMatch?
Sign up free with your name, mobile number and trade, pick your category and area, and contact hirers directly on WhatsApp. Agree the rate and timing on WhatsApp before starting work.

Do I have to pay any agent or commission?
No. There is no agent in between and GigMatch takes no commission. You deal directly with the hirer.

How is the pay rate decided?
The rate is not fixed by GigMatch. You and the hirer agree it on WhatsApp, based on the work, area and experience, before the job starts.

Which areas of Lahore are covered?
All across Lahore, including Gulberg, Johar Town, DHA, Model Town, Shahdara, Wapda Town, Bahria Town, Lahore Cantt, Township, Faisal Town, Iqbal Town and surrounding areas. Nearby matches are shown first.
