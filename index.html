How It Works

Sirf 3 steps — ghar bethay kaam shuru

Na lambi form-bazi, na agent ke chakkar. Signup karo, qareebi match dekho, WhatsApp par baat kar lo.

1
Signup karo

WhatsApp par direct baat

FAQ

Aksar poochay janay walay sawal

GigMatch ke baray mein jo sab se zyada poocha jata hai — seedha jawab.

GigMatch PK kya hai?

GigMatch PK Pakistan ka apna rozgar app hai. Yeh un logon ko jorta hai jinhein kaam chahiye (mazdoor, painter, plumber, electrician, factory worker, driver, student waghera) un logon se jinhein banda chahiye (ghar walay, dukaan dar, factory owners, businesses). Rabta seedha WhatsApp par hota hai — beech mein koi agent nahi.

Mujhe mazdoor chahiye, main kya karun?

App kholo, "Banda Chahiye" select karo aur Mazdoor category choose karo. Tumhari location ke hisaab se qareeb tareen mazdoor sab se pehle nazar ayenge. Jo pasand aye, us ke card par WhatsApp button dabao — seedha usi se baat karo aur kaam tay kar lo.

Kaam chahiye to kya karun?

"Kaam Chahiye" select karke signup karo — naam, mobile number aur apna kaam (mazdoor, painter, loader, call center, jo bhi ho). OTP WhatsApp par ayega. Profile ban jaye to tumhare qareeb ki jobs nazar ana shuru ho jayengi, aur hirers tumhein khud bhi WhatsApp par rabta kar sakenge.

Kya signup free hai?

Ji haan — signup 100% free hai. Na registration fees, na commission. Worker aur hirer seedha apas mein baat karte hain, GigMatch beech mein koi paisa nahi leta.

Rate kaise tay hota hai?

Rate likhna lazmi nahi — profile aur job baghair rate ke bhi ban jati hai. Asal rate dono banday WhatsApp par baat karke khud tay karte hain, bilkul waisay jaisay aam taur par hota hai. GigMatch rate fix nahi karta.

Rabta kaise hota hai?

Rabta sirf WhatsApp par hota hai. Har worker aur job card par number nazar ata hai aur ek bara green WhatsApp button hota hai — button dabao, chat khul jayegi. WhatsApp se message bhi ho jayega aur call bhi. App ke andar alag chat system nahi hai.
