GUIDE — BANDA CHAHIYE

Mazdoor Chahiye? Lahore Mein Banda Dhundne Ka Asaan Tareeqa

Agar aap ko Lahore mein mazdoor chahiye — ghar ki tameer, shifting, safai, loading ya kisi bhi kaam ke liye — to ye guide aap ke liye hai. Banda dhundne ke puranay tareeqay (chowk par jana, jan pehchan se poochna) waqt zaya karte hain. GigMatch par aap apne apne area ke mazdoor se seedha WhatsApp par baat kar sakte hain.

Lahore Mein Mazdoor Ki Dihadi Kitni Hai?

Aam mazdoor (helper)Rs 1,200 – 1,800 / din
Tajurba-kaar mazdoorRs 1,800 – 2,500 / din
Raj mistriRs 2,500 – 3,500 / din
Loader (saman uthana / shifting)Rs 1,500 – 2,500 / din

Banda Dhundte Waqt 5 Zaroori Batain

  • Rate pehle tay karein — kaam shuru hone se pehle dihadi ya theka saaf likh/bata dein.
  • Qareeb ka banda chunein — apne area ka mazdoor waqt par ata hai aur kiraya bhi bachata hai.
  • Kaam ki tafseel batayein — kitne din, kya kaam, auzaar kis ke — sab pehle clear karein.
  • Chhote kaam se shuru karein — naye bande ko pehle aik din ka kaam de kar dekh lein.
  • WhatsApp par baat karein — number aur baat cheet mehfooz rehti hai.

GigMatch Par Mazdoor Kaise Milega?

App kholein, "Banda Chahiye" chunein, apna area (masalan Gulberg, Johar Town, Shahdara) select karein — qareeb tareen mazdooron ki list samne aa jayegi. Jo pasand aye, us ke WhatsApp button se seedha baat karein. Koi agent nahi, koi commission nahi.
