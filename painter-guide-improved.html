Painter Ki Qismein — Kaun Sa Banda Kis Kaam Ke Liye?

"Painter" aik lafz hai, lekin kaam ke hisaab se bande alag hote hain. Kaam batate waqt saaf likh dein ke kis qism ka kaam hai:

  • Dihadi painter — rozana dihadi par kaam karne wala tajurba-kaar rang wala. Aik kamra, aik deewar ya chhota kaam ho to yehi behtar hota hai.
  • Theka painter — poora ghar ya bara kaam per sq ft theke par lene wala. Kul kharcha pehle se tay ho jata hai, aur kaam mukammal karne ki zimmedari usi ki hoti hai.
  • Putty aur polish specialist — purani deewaron ki putty, crack bharai, aur polish/lacquer ka kaam. Lakri ke darwazon aur furniture ki polish ke liye alag banda chahiye hota hai.
  • Texture aur design painter — texture paint, stencil ya feature wall jaisa khaas kaam. Aam painter se mehenga hota hai, lekin aik deewar poore kamray ki shakal badal deti hai.
  • Painter ka helper — painter ke saath kaam karne wala madadgaar: saman uthana, safai, purana rang chheelna aur coat ke darmiyan tayyari. Helper ki dihadi painter se kam hoti hai.

Kaam ki tafseel pehle se saaf ho to banda bhi sahi milta hai aur rate bhi pehle tay ho jata hai. "Painter chahiye" kehne se behtar hai ye likhna: "2 kamron ka rang karwana hai, Johar Town, putty bhi karni hai, 3 din ka kaam."

Dihadi Ya Theka — Kaunsa Behtar Hai?

Ye faisla kaam ke size par hai:

  • Dihadi — aik kamra, aik deewar, ya 1–2 din ka chhota kaam. Aap khud maujood reh kar kaam dekh sakte hain, aur din ke hisaab se payment hoti hai.
  • Theka (per sq ft) — poora ghar, flat ya bara kaam. Kul kharcha pehle se pata chal jata hai, aur painter ko kaam jaldi mukammal karne ki jaldi hoti hai. Theka ho to kaam ki hadd pehle likh lein: kitne kamray, putty shamil hai ya nahi, kitne coat lagenge, aur kab tak mukammal hoga.

Jo bhi tareeqa chunein, aik baat hamesha yaad rakhein: rate WhatsApp par likh kar tay karein — zubani rate baad mein bhool jata hai, likha hua rate behes se bachata hai.

Paint Karwane Se Pehle Ye Tay Karein

Painter bulane se pehle ye 5 batain tay kar lein — aadha masla wahin hal ho jata hai:

  1. Kitna area rang hoga — kitne kamray, kitni deewarein, chhat bhi rangni hai ya nahi? Theka per sq ft hai ya dihadi?
  2. Deewaron ki halat — putty, crack bharai aur safai shamil hai ya nahi? Purani deewar par pehle putty aur primer lagta hai, warna naya rang jaldi kharab ho jata hai.
  3. Rang konsi company ka aur kitne coat lagenge? — aam tor par 2 coat lagte hain; aik coat sasta padta hai lekin rang ki chamak aur mazbooti kam hoti hai.
  4. Kaam kitne din mein mukammal hoga? — aik kamra 1–2 din, poora ghar aam tor par 4–7 din. Coat ke darmiyan sukhne ka waqt bhi ginna chahiye.
  5. Saman kis ka? — seerhi, brush aur roller zyada tar painter apna lata hai; rang aur putty aam tor par ghar walay ka hota hai. Pehle pooch lein.

Rang Ki Qismein — Konsa Rang Kahan Lagta Hai?

Har jagah aik hi rang nahi lagta. Aam tor par:

  • Emulsion (andar ki deewarein) — kamron aur drawing room ke liye sab se aam; dhone layak (washable) emulsion mehenga lekin saaf karna aasan hota hai.
  • Distemper — sasta rang, kiraye ke ghar ya aarzi kaam ke liye; emulsion jitna mazboot nahi hota.
  • Weather shield / bahar wala rang — ghar ki bahar ki deewaron ke liye, dhoop aur baarish se bachata hai.
  • Enamel (lakri aur loha) — darwaze, khirkiyan aur grill ke liye; chamakdar aur mazboot hota hai.

Rang khareedte waqt dukaan se receipt zaroor lein aur dibba kholne se pehle rang ka naam aur shade note kar lein — dobara rang lena ho to wahi shade mil jaye.

Sahi Painter Chunnay Ki 7 Tips

  1. Rate pehle tay karein — kaam shuru hone se pehle dihadi ya theka saaf likh ya bata dein. WhatsApp par likha hua rate baad mein behes se bachata hai.
  2. Pehle kaam dekh lein — painter se poochein ke us ne aisa kaam pehle kiya hai ya nahi. Aik kamra ya aik deewar pehle rangwa kar dekh lein, phir poora ghar dein.
  3. Qareeb ka banda chunein — apne area ka painter waqt par ata hai aur kiraya bhi bachata hai.
  4. Putty aur primer mat chhorein — purani deewar par seedha rang lagana paise zaya karna hai. Pehle putty, phir primer, phir rang — yehi sahi tareeqa hai.
  5. Coat ki taadad likh lein — 1 coat ya 2 coat, ye rate mein farq dalta hai. Theka ho to coat ki taadad pehle likh lein.
  6. Kaam roz check karein — har din ke end par kaam dekh lein. Aik deewar kharab ho to usi waqt theek karwana aasan hota hai, poora ghar rangne ke baad nahi.
  7. Payment kaam ke baad — rate pehle tay karein, aur poori payment kaam mukammal aur check karne ke baad dein. Advance dena ho to chhoti raqam dein aur likh kar rakhein.

Aksar Pooche Janay Walay Sawal

Aik kamray ka rang kitne mein hoga?
Size aur deewar ki halat par hai — aam tor par painter pehle kamra dekh kar hi pakka rate deta hai. Labour theka aam tor par Rs 25–45 per sq ft hota hai, aur rang ka kharcha hamesha alag hota hai.

Painter ki dihadi Lahore mein kitni hai?
2026 mein aik tajurba-kaar painter ki dihadi aam tor par Rs 2,000–3,000 hoti hai, aur helper ki Rs 1,200–1,500. Asal rate kaam, area aur tajurbay ke hisaab se hota hai.

Painter apna saman (seerhi, brush) lata hai?
Zyada tar painters apne auzaar (seerhi, brush, roller) late hain; rang aur putty aam tor par ghar walay ka hota hai — kaam shuru karne se pehle pooch lein.

Rang ka kharcha alag kyun hota hai?
Rang ki qeemat company, quality aur coat ki taadad par hoti hai, is liye woh labour se alag ginna jata hai — ya to aap khud khareed kar dein, ya painter ki zimmedari mein theka dein. Dono sooraton mein rate pehle likh kar tay karna behtar hai.

Theka behtar hai ya dihadi?
Aik kamra ya chhota kaam dihadi par behtar hota hai; poora ghar ya bara kaam theke (per sq ft) par behtar hota hai, kyunke kul kharcha pehle se pata chal jata hai. Theka ho to kaam ki hadd pehle likh lein.

Poora ghar rang karwane mein kitne din lagte hain?
Aik kamra 1–2 din mein ho jata hai; poora ghar aam tor par 4–7 din leta hai — deewaron ki halat, putty ka kaam aur coat ki taadad par hai. Painter se kaam shuru karne se pehle waqt tay kar lein.
