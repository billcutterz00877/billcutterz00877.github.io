Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

GigMatch PK ("GigMatch", "hum", "hamari") aap ki privacy ki hifazat ke liye pur-azm hai. Ye policy batati hai ke GigMatch app aur website (gigmatch.site) istemal karte waqt hum konsi maloomat ikatthi karte hain, unhein kaise istemal karte hain, aur aap ke paas kya ikhtiyarat hain.

1. Hum Konsi Maloomat Ikatthi Karte Hain

Account banate waqt hum sirf wahi maloomat lete hain jo matching ke liye zaroori hain:

  • Naam — aap ki profile par dikhane ke liye.
  • Mobile / WhatsApp number — signup verification (OTP) aur doosre users se rabte ke liye. GigMatch par rabta WhatsApp ke zariye hota hai, is liye aap ka number match honay walay users ko nazar ata hai.
  • Sheher aur area (city/area) — taake aap ko qareeb tareen workers aur jobs pehle dikhaye ja saken.
  • Category — aap ka kaam ya darkaar category (masalan mazdoor, painter, plumber, electrician, factory worker waghera).
  • Rate (optional) — agar aap likhna chahein to; ye bilkul ikhtiyari hai.

Hum bank details, card numbers ya koi mali (financial) maloomat ikatthi nahi karte.

2. Maloomat Ka Istemal

  • Workers aur hirers ke darmiyan matching ke liye — category aur location ki bunyaad par.
  • Signup ke waqt WhatsApp par OTP bhejne ke liye.
  • App ki khidmat behtar banane aur misuse (spam, fake profiles) rokne ke liye.

Hum aap ki zaati maloomat kisi teesray fareeq (third party) ko bechte nahi hain.

3. Maloomat Kis Ke Sath Share Hoti Hai

Aap ki profile (naam, category, sheher/area aur WhatsApp number) GigMatch ke doosre registered users ko nazar ati hai, taake woh aap se direct rabta kar saken — yehi app ka bunyadi maqsad hai. Is ke ilawa maloomat sirf usi waqt share hogi jab qanoon is ka taqaza kare.

4. Data Ki Hifazat

Hum aap ki maloomat ko mehfooz rakhne ke liye munasib technical aur organizational tadabeer istemal karte hain. Taham internet par koi bhi tareeqa 100% mehfooz nahi hota; aap apna OTP kisi ke sath share na karein.

5. Aap Ke Huqooq

  • Aap kisi bhi waqt apni profile maloomat dekh aur tabdeel kar sakte hain.
  • Aap apna account aur maloomat delete karwane ki darkhwast kar sakte hain.
  • Account band karne se aap ki profile doosre users ko nazar ana band ho jati hai.

6. Bachon Ki Privacy

GigMatch 18 saal se kam umar afraad ke liye nahi hai. Agar humein maloom ho ke kisi kam-umar ka data galti se ikattha ho gaya hai, to hum use delete kar denge.

7. Policy Mein Tabdeeli

Hum waqtan fawaqtan ye policy update kar sakte hain. Nayi policy is page par post ki jayegi aur "Last updated" ki tareekh tabdeel kar di jayegi.

8. Rabta

Privacy se mutaliq kisi bhi sawal ya darkhwast ke liye hum se website gigmatch.site ke zariye rabta karein.
