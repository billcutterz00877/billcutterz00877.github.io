Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

GigMatch PK ("GigMatch", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the GigMatch app and website (gigmatch.site), how we use it, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect only the information needed for matching:

  • Name — to display on your profile.
  • Mobile / WhatsApp number — for signup verification (OTP) and for contact with other users. Contact on GigMatch happens via WhatsApp, so your number is visible to users you match with.
  • City and area — so that the nearest workers and jobs can be shown to you first.
  • Category — your work or required category (for example labourer, painter, plumber, electrician, factory worker, and others).
  • Rate (optional) — only if you choose to write it; this is entirely optional.

We do not collect bank details, card numbers, or any other financial information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To match workers and hirers — on the basis of category and location.
  • To send an OTP via WhatsApp at signup.
  • To improve the app's service and to prevent misuse (spam, fake profiles).

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Who Information Is Shared With

Your profile (name, category, city/area, and WhatsApp number) is visible to other registered GigMatch users, so that they can contact you directly — this is the app's core purpose. Apart from this, information will only be shared when the law requires it.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep your information safe. However, no method on the internet is 100% secure; do not share your OTP with anyone.

5. Your Rights

  • You can view and change your profile information at any time.
  • You can request deletion of your account and information.
  • Closing your account stops your profile from being visible to other users.

6. Children's Privacy

GigMatch is not for people under 18 years of age. If we learn that a minor's data was collected by mistake, we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new policy will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related question or request, contact us via the website gigmatch.site.
