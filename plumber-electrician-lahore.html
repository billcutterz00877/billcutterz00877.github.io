GUIDE — PLUMBER & ELECTRICIAN

Plumber ya Electrician Chahiye Lahore Mein?

Naal band ho gaya, pipe leak hai, ya bijli ka kaam par gaya? Lahore mein plumber aur electrician ki sab se zyada zarurat achanak padti hai. Ye guide aap ko aam rates aur sahi banda chunnay ka tareeqa batati hai.

Plumber aur Electrician Ke Aam Rates

Chhota kaam (naal, toti, switch waghera)Rs 500 – 1,500
Plumber ki dihadi (bara kaam)Rs 2,000 – 3,000 / din
Electrician ki dihadiRs 2,000 – 3,000 / din
Bathroom / kitchen ki mukammal fittingEstimate pehle — rate tay kar ke

Sahi Banda Chunnay Ki Tips

  • Bare kaam ka estimate pehle lein — banda ghar aa kar kaam dekhe, phir rate tay ho.
  • Bijli ke kaam mein tajurba lazmi — wiring aur board ka kaam hamesha tajurba-kaar electrician se karwayein.
  • Saman ki list pehle banwayein — pipe, fitting ya cable jo lage, pehle likh lein taake hisaab saaf rahe.
  • Apne area ka banda — masla dobara ho to qareeb wala foran aa jata hai.
