GUIDE — DRIVER

Driver Chahiye Lahore Mein?

Roz office jana hai, bachon ko school chhorna hai ya company ke liye driver chahiye — driver chahiye Lahore mein? Acha driver milna ittefaq nahi, sahi check-list ka nateeja hota hai. Ye guide aap ko wohi check-list deti hai.

Driver ke maamlay mein sab se bara masla baad mein pata chalta hai — is liye license, timing aur gaari ka sawal pehle din hi clear kar lein.

Driver Hire Karne Ki 5 Zaroori Batain

  • License aur CNIC pehle dekhein — license ki expiry aur asli hona check karein, photo bhi le lein.
  • Short trial drive lein — pehle din aik chhota trip sath kar ke driving aur bartao dono dekh lein.
  • Route ka test lein — apne roz ke rastay (office, school, market) par driver ko aik dafa le jayein.
  • Duty timing likh kar dein — roz aane ka waqt, chhutti ka din, overtime ka usool — sab pehle clear ho.
  • Apne area ka driver — qareeb rehne wala driver waqt par ata hai aur emergency mein bhi kaam ata hai. Lahore ke areas: DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, Wapda Town, Shahdara, Askari, LDA Avenue, Valencia Town, Etihad Town.

Aksar Pooche Janay Walay Sawal

Monthly driver mil jayega ya sirf daily?
Dono — mustaqil ghar ya office ke liye monthly driver, aur aik din ya aik trip ke liye daily driver. Timing aur chhutti pehle tay kar lein.

Driver ki gaari hogi ya meri gaari chalaye ga?
Ye sab se pehla sawal hai — aksar driver aap ki gaari chalata hai. Agar driver ke paas apni gaari chahiye to ye pehle saaf poochein.

Night duty ya out of city trip ke liye driver milega?
Ji haan, agar waqt aur manzil pehle tay ho — lambi trip ka rate aur wapas aane ka waqt pehle se clear kar lein.
