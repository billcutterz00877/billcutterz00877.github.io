Loader Chahiye Lahore Mein?

Ghar badal rahe hain, dukaan ka saman uthana hai ya godam mein loading karwani hai — loader chahiye Lahore mein? Sahi loader milna mushkil nahi, bas saman aur waqt ki tafseel pehle saaf honi chahiye. Ye guide aap ko wohi batati hai.

Loader Hire Karne Ki 5 Zaroori Batain

  • Saman ki list pehle bata dein — almari, fridge, bed, dabay — taake loader ko andaza ho kitne bande chahiye.
  • Nazuk cheezain alag batayein — sheesha, TV, crockery — in ki packing aur uthanay ka tareeqa pehle samjha dein.
  • Manzil aur lift check karein — lift hai ya seerhi se uthana hai, ye rate aur waqt dono badal deta hai.
  • Waqt fix karein — subah kitne baje aana hai, ye pehle tay ho to shifting din bhar nahi khinchti.
  • Apne area ka banda — qareeb ka loader waqt par ata hai. Lahore ke areas: DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Bahria Town, Wapda Town, Shahdara, Askari, LDA Avenue, Valencia Town, Etihad Town.

Aksar Pooche Janay Walay Sawal

Aik loader kafi hoga ya zyada chahiye?
Saman par hai — aik kamray ka saman aam tor par aik do loader utha lete hain, puray ghar ki shifting ke liye zyada bande chahiye hote hain. Saman ki list pehle bata dein.

Gaari bhi mil sakti hai ya sirf loader?
Loader ka kaam saman uthana hai; gaari (suzuki, shahzor) ka bandobast pehle se pooch lein taake shifting wale din koi rukawat na ho.

Raat ko shifting ho sakti hai?
Ji haan, agar waqt pehle tay ho — lekin raat ke kaam ka waqt aur rate pehle se clear kar lein.
