Rang (paint) ka kharcha hamesha alag hota hai — ya to aap khud khareed kar dein, ya painter ki zimmedari mein theka dein. Dono sooraton mein rate pehle likh kar tay karna behtar hai.

Paint Karwane Se Pehle Ye Tay Karein

  • Kitne kamray / kitna area rang hoga — theka per sq ft hai ya dihadi?
  • Deewaron ki halat — putty, crack bharai aur safai shamil hai ya nahi?
  • Rang konsi company ka aur kitne coat lagenge?
  • Kaam kitne din mein mukammal hoga?
